# Connect Tally

Create a free account at https://tally.markets/sign-up, then follow https://tally.markets/connect for your client.

Remote endpoint: `https://tally.markets/api/mcp`; Streamable HTTP, OAuth authentication.

After connecting, ask “Show me how Tally works with a sample trade.” Compare two and three contracts in the fictional example, then check your own trade. No journal entry or brokerage connection is required. If you already have a trade in mind, say “Use Tally to check this trade’s size and what I could lose.” The example's facts never become your trade's facts.

For returning users with saved work: “Review my saved Tally ideas. What changed, and what needs my attention?”

Your plans and their evidence are together at https://tally.markets/workspace. Discuss a trade in your AI app; Tally can save the plan and attach a supported watch after one confirmation. The thesis is why the trade should work, inside the larger game plan. You do not need to complete a thesis questionnaire.

If Claude still shows the older tools, open Customize → Connectors → Tally → More options → Refresh tools list. If ChatGPT has multiple Tally accounts connected, name the account you want to use; paid access belongs to that Tally account, not to the ChatGPT subscription. Client permissions may add a tool-approval prompt. Ordinary checks and examples stay in the host conversation, with native reply choices when available. Ask for a visual or interactive view to open a branded Tally card. Typed replies always work; host controls and permission prompts vary.

Free: 3 active trade plans, including drafts, and 10 total created. Closing frees an active slot but does not reset the total. Existing history remains readable, reviewable and closable. Sentinel has no creation cap and supports up to 3 active evidence watches; Connected supports up to 10. Background watching requires active paid-tier access. See the current pricing on the website.

The initial company-evidence watch covers Circle USDC flows and selected company disclosures. The preview states the actual coverage, end date and notification settings. Option-value and expiry coverage requires a separately configured qualified feed; supplying contract details alone does not enable it. First observations are silent baselines, not evidence that the trade is improving.

No tool executes trades or changes brokerage positions. Creating, arming and closing a thesis are journal actions. Existing optional Propr accounts are accessible under Settings, separately from the journal. Never confuse a journal close with a position close.
